From your price list to a bot

What it takes to have one of these answering for your own business.

  1. You send what you offer

    Your services and prices, your team, your opening hours, and the questions customers ask most. A message or a photo of your price list is enough.

  2. We set up your bot

    Your own Telegram bot, with your name and picture, speaking the way your customers write — Lebanese, Saudi, Arabizi, English or French.

  3. It answers, day and night

    Prices, opening hours and questions are answered on the spot. Bookings and orders are taken in the chat, without a call.

  4. You hear about every one

    Each new booking, change, cancellation, order or message arrives in your own Telegram, with the customer's name and number.
